Delete your BreakLoop account

BreakLoop by Suraj Gupta · com.breakloop.app

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open BreakLoop.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all its data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: without the app

Send an email to the developer email address shown on BreakLoop’s Google Play listing, from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject “Delete my BreakLoop account”. We’ll delete your account and confirm within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and account ID) and profile answers.
  • Your limited apps and allowances, exercise sessions, minutes history, daily totals and Journey progress.
  • Leaderboards you own, your memberships and your Duels attempts, and invite-code redemptions.

What is kept

Nothing is kept in our database after deletion. Copies in routine backups, if any, are removed when those backups expire, within 30 days. Data stored only on your phone stays there until you uninstall BreakLoop.
