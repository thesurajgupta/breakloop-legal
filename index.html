BreakLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026 · Applies to the BreakLoop Android app (com.breakloop.app)

In short: BreakLoop limits the apps you choose and unlocks them when you do push-ups or squats. Your camera video is processed on your phone and never recorded or uploaded. We never read what you see or type in other apps. We don’t sell data and we don’t use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Who we are

BreakLoop is developed by Suraj Gupta (“we”). For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the developer email address shown on BreakLoop’s Google Play listing.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Which app is on screen. BreakLoop’s Accessibility service reads only the package name of the app currently on screen, to count time in the apps you chose to limit and to show the BreakLoop lock when their free minutes run out. It does not read text, images or input inside other apps and does not tap or type for you.
  • Usage statistics. If you grant Usage Access, BreakLoop reads how long you used apps, to show your real screen time and keep limits accurate.
  • Camera frames. Used only to count your reps with Google MediaPipe on the device. Frames are never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Your installed apps list. Used only on the device so you can choose which apps to limit.

Data stored in your account

When you create an account, we store the following with our database provider so you can restore your plan on a new phone and use Duels:

  • Email address and account ID.
  • The answers you gave during setup: display name, age, goals, habits, current and target screen time, fitness level and preferred exercise.
  • The apps you chose to limit (app name and package name) and their daily allowances.
  • Exercise sessions (exercise type, reps, time, form score), your minutes balance history and daily totals, including minutes spent in limited apps.
  • Journey progress, leaderboards you create or join, your display name and attempt scores shown to members of those leaderboards, and invite-code redemptions.

How we use it

Only to provide BreakLoop’s features: enforcing your limits, counting reps, restoring your account, streaks, Journey and Duels. We don’t use your data for advertising or profiling, and we don’t sell it.

Sharing and service providers

Account data is stored by Supabase (database and authentication), acting on our behalf. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us. Your display name and Duels scores are visible to members of leaderboards you join. We share nothing else with third parties.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on the device using the Android Keystore. Access to account data is restricted per user by database row-level security.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion from our database is immediate; copies in routine backups, if any, are removed when those backups expire, within 30 days. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall BreakLoop.

Your choices

You can turn off the Accessibility service, Usage Access, notifications or camera access at any time in Android settings. While protection is off, your limits are not enforced.

Children

BreakLoop is intended for people aged 13 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
